## English

BLECH 1.3.5 and the app 1.3.5.

**New**
- The app now speaks English, French, Italian, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish, Czech, Russian and Ukrainian. On first start it uses your system's language; you can change it in the settings.
- Samples over the cable: in the Samples tab, read the samples on the card, add WAVs and remove samples without taking the card out of the Electribe. "Save as file" copies the whole `e2sSample.all` to your computer.
- Plugins can write steps into the sequencer (`korg.seq`, API 9), for example for arpeggios and melody generators. The plugin docs have an example.
- PLUGIN EDIT lists every plugin, the loaded ones and the ones on the card. You start a plugin from the card right there; IMPORT PLUGIN is gone.
- Display and LED colours are set with HUE, SAT and BRIGHT instead of red, green and blue. Older settings stay as they are.
- METER STILL can now be OFF.
- Two new insert presets: Slap Delay and Long Delay.
- Samples load about 12 percent faster at start.

**Fixed**
- Scrolling through the menu past Blechy's page left bits of Blechy on the display.
- On slow computers the animated background sometimes stopped right at the app's start.
