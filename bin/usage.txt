e2: tools for Korg Electribe 2 effect presets

  e2 ls       <SYSTEM.VSB> [mfx|ifx]        list the presets in a firmware image
  e2 show     <SYSTEM.VSB> <mfx|ifx> <slot> one preset in full
  e2 lint     <SYSTEM.VSB> [mfx|ifx] [slot] check presets against everything
                                            that has gone wrong before
  e2 profile  <SYSTEM.VSB>                  learn the factory value ranges,
                                            which the linter then uses
  e2 selftest <SYSTEM.VSB>                  drive the simulated device and show
                                            what it refuses and why

The firmware image is yours. Nothing here writes to it, and no part of it is
stored in this repository.
